    Do subagents burn your Claude Code limit faster?

    Measured on a real account: a subagent call costs about the same share of the 5-hour limit as a main-thread call. A typical subagent costs about 2% of a window; five in parallel, about 10%.

  • 25 October 2026

    How many tokens is 1% of your Claude Code limit?

    Measured from two days of real Claude Code readings: 1% of the 5-hour limit is about 166k new tokens or 11 million cached ones. New tokens were 2.5% of what I used and about 63% of the limit.
