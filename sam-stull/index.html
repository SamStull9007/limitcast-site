Sam Stull

Sam Stull builds Limitcast and writes the notes on this site.

Limitcast is a free local tool that forecasts usage limits for Claude Code, Codex, and Grok, and trims context those tools load before you type. The measurements and release notes are on the blog. The tool and this site are independent of Rillion, Anthropic, and OpenAI.

Sam is Vice President of Sales at Rillion in Austin. His work there is accounts payable automation and B2B payments.
