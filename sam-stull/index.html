Sam Stull

Vice President of Sales, North America at Rillion. B2B SaaS, finance technology, and teams built for growth.

Sam leads North American sales at Rillion, hiring and coaching quota-carrying account executives focused on net-new SaaS revenue. His team works with CFOs, controllers, and AP leaders at mid-market and enterprise organizations evaluating accounts payable automation and supplier payments.

Sales leadership across three markets

Before Rillion, Sam led sales at GCPay, a construction payments platform. Sales increased more than 300% after he took over the team. Former direct report Dave O’Holleran describes that result, and Sam’s approach to coaching and supporting sellers, in a recommendation on Sam’s LinkedIn profile.

At QuestCDN, Sam served as Vice President of Sales and Marketing, working with government procurement teams and contractors on digital bidding and plan-room software. His work included a landmark contract with the State of Minnesota.

Based in Austin, Texas, Sam holds an MBA in Finance from LeTourneau University. Connect with him on LinkedIn to discuss SaaS sales leadership, team development, or finance automation.

Building Limitcast

Sam also builds Limitcast and writes the notes on this site. Limitcast is a free local tool that forecasts usage limits for Claude Code, Codex, and Grok, and trims context those tools load before you type. The measurements and release notes are on the blog. The tool and this site are independent of Rillion, Anthropic, and OpenAI.
